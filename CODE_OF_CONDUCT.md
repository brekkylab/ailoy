# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in Ailoy — filing an issue, reviewing a patch, answering a question on
Discord — is welcome regardless of experience, background, or identity, and can expect to be
treated with respect.

## What we expect

- Review the work, not the person. "This turn drops the tool result, here is the test that shows
  it" is the argument; "you clearly didn't read the code" is not.
- Accept correction gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Assume good faith about intent, and ask before concluding.
- Keep it public where it can be public, so the reasoning stays with the code.

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and
persistent unwelcome attention are not acceptable here, in any venue where you represent this
project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainers at oceanjoon@brekkylab.com. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared with the person reported.

Maintainers will respond as they judge appropriate: editing or removing the content, a warning,
or a temporary or permanent ban from participating. Maintainers who do not uphold this document
in good faith are subject to it themselves.
