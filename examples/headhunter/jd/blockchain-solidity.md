# Smart Contract Engineer (Solidity)

## About the team

Ridgeline is building settlement rails on-chain. The contracts will hold customer funds
from day one, so we are looking for someone who has shipped contracts that already did.

## What you will do

- Write and audit Solidity contracts for a settlement protocol
- Own the upgrade path — proxy patterns, storage layout, migration safety
- Work with our security firm through audit cycles

## Requirements

- Solidity in production: contracts you wrote that held funds
- Familiarity with common vulnerability classes and how they are found
- 3+ years of engineering experience

## Nice to have

- Formal verification or fuzzing experience
- EVM internals
- Prior audit engagement, on either side

## Details

- Full-time, remote within ±3 hours of CET
- Process: résumé review → contract review exercise → two interviews
