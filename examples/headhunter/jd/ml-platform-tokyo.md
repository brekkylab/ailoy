# ML Platform Engineer — Tokyo

## About the team

Sundial runs the training and serving infrastructure that four modeling teams share.
The platform started as one person's scheduler scripts and is now load-bearing for the
whole company, which is the problem — it works, and nobody wants to touch it.

## What you will do

- Own GPU scheduling on Kubernetes. Jobs currently queue behind each other in ways the
  submitting team cannot see or predict
- Rebuild the training orchestration layer for PyTorch jobs so that a run is
  reproducible from its inputs
- Take over the feature store the modeling teams read from

## Requirements

- Kubernetes, MLOps, and PyTorch — the role sits where all three meet
- Experience owning a platform other teams depended on
- Based in Tokyo

## Nice to have

- GPU scheduling or capacity planning at scale
- Feature store or data platform work
- Experience taking over a system somebody else built

## Details

- Full-time, on-site in Tokyo (Shibuya)
- Process: résumé review → two technical interviews → team session
